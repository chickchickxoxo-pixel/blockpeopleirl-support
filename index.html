Common questions

Before you email

What does the app do?

Create Avoid Profiles using a nickname or emoji, save areas and public places you prefer to keep clear of, and use your selected profiles when viewing a map or planning a route. The app does not find or track other people, and cannot guarantee that you will avoid an encounter.

How do profile purchases work?

Your first two profiles are free. Each additional profile requires one in-app purchase credit. One credit has a US reference price of $0.99; the App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm. This is a consumable purchase, not a subscription. Editing profiles and using routes remain free.

What if a purchase succeeds but a profile is not saved?

Return to the Avoid List and use Retry Save / Purchase if it is shown. Avoid confirming another purchase if you believe you have already paid. If the profile still does not save, email support with the app version, what happened, and the Apple transaction reference if available. Never send your password or payment-card details.

How do I show my current location?

Open Avoid Map and choose Share in the location confirmation, then allow location access in the iOS prompt. If you previously denied access, open Settings on your iPhone, find the app, and check Location permissions. You can also enter a starting point manually for a route.

Why is a route or place not available?

Check your internet connection and select a matching result for the starting point and destination. Map and routing coverage depends on external services and can vary by region and travel mode. If it still fails, send support the steps you took and your app version. You can omit or blur private locations.

Where are my profiles and credits stored?

Profiles and purchase-credit records are stored on this device. Removing the app can remove this data. The app does not offer an account-based cloud backup or promise automatic restoration of consumable credits after reinstalling. To delete a profile, use Remove in Avoid List; Clear All Saved Profiles removes all saved profiles.

How do I ask about a charge or refund?

For an Apple purchase, you can check your purchase history or request a refund through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For an issue inside the app, email support. Apple handles the payment and any refund decision.

This app is a personal planning tool based on places you enter. It is not an emergency service and does not determine anyone else’s current location.
