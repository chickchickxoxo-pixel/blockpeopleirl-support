Privacy Policy

How block people in real life handles information when you use the app or contact support.

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

1. Who we are

block people in real life is provided by Honoka Kumagai. For privacy questions or requests, contact blockpeopleirl.support@gmail.com. The app does not require you to create an account.

2. Profiles stored on your device

The app stores information you enter in Avoid Profiles, such as a nickname or emoji, country, areas, stations, public places, and interests where provided. These records are stored locally on your device so you can view, edit, and use them for planning. Use a nickname or emoji rather than unnecessary identifying information about another person.

Saving a profile does not create a public profile or locate another person. Searches, suggestions, and route requests send the information needed for those features to network services, as described below.

3. Location, searches, suggestions, and routes

  • Your current location: with your permission, the app accesses your device's location to show you on the map or use it as a route starting point. Location access is requested while using the app. The app does not use background location tracking.
  • Place searches: search text and relevant geographic parameters are sent to the Photon place-search service at photon.komoot.io to find matching areas and public places.
  • Suggestions: when the online suggestion feature is used, country, city or area text, and interests or preference text are sent to the app's API hosted on Render. When AI generation is available, this context is sent to OpenAI to generate broad place-category suggestions. The current suggestion request does not include the profile nickname or emoji, income, or the saved favorite-place list. Avoid entering names, private addresses, or sensitive personal information in preference text. If online generation is unavailable, the app may display categories generated on your device.
  • Route planning: requests can include starting and destination text and coordinates, travel mode, departure or arrival date and time, a time-zone offset, intermediate coordinates, and the coordinates and radii of selected avoid areas or places. This information is sent to the app's API hosted on Render and the routing services used to calculate a route. Direct routing requests may also use routing.openstreetmap.de or router.project-osrm.org.
  • Maps and external navigation: Apple Maps displays map content on iOS. If you open a route or place in an external map service, such as Apple Maps or Google Maps, the relevant location or route parameters are passed to that service.

Network services receive technical information needed to process a request, such as an IP address and request metadata. Requests are not anonymous merely because the app has no sign-in. Geographic coordinates can reveal a precise location. Search text, preferences, and location information may identify a person when combined with other information available to a service provider.

4. In-app purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. The app keeps local credit balances and transaction identifiers to apply purchased credits and avoid applying the same transaction twice. We do not receive your full payment-card number from Apple. Apple handles its purchase records under its own privacy policy.

5. Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Support email is handled through Gmail. Please do not send passwords, card numbers, or unnecessary personal information.

This support website does not include an advertising tracker, analytics script, or a form that collects profile data. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure the website, according to its privacy statement.

6. Service providers and sharing

Information is shared with the services involved in the feature you use: Apple for maps and purchases; Photon/komoot for place searches; Render for hosting the app's API; OpenAI for online AI suggestions; and the search and routing providers used by the API. Depending on service configuration and the route requested, routing providers may include OpenStreetMap-based services, Google, NAVITIME through RapidAPI, HERE, Geoapify, or Stadia Maps. Google also processes information when you open Google Maps or use Gmail for support correspondence. GitHub Pages hosts this website.

We use this information for app functionality, personalized category suggestions, purchase-credit handling, support, security, and operation of these services. We do not sell Avoid Profiles or location information, or use them for advertising. We do not use these data for cross-app advertising tracking.

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their terms, privacy notices, and applicable service settings govern their handling of requests and records.

Relevant notices include:

  • Apple Privacy
  • komoot Privacy
  • Render Privacy
  • OpenAI Privacy and OpenAI API data controls
  • FOSSGIS Privacy
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy
  • Google Privacy
  • GitHub Privacy

We may also disclose information when required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Local profiles remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app's data. Use Remove or Clear All Saved Profiles in Avoid List to delete saved profiles. Removing a profile does not erase an Apple purchase record or automatically refund a purchase. Local purchase-credit records may be removed when the app is deleted; consumable credits are not an account-based cloud balance.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and related purchase or technical issues. You can email support to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

The app API writes operational logs without request bodies, search text, coordinates, IP addresses, or full request URLs. These logs include request identifiers, status codes, and processing times. The API temporarily processes IP addresses in memory for rate limiting. Render dashboard logs are available for 7 days under our current Hobby workspace plan. This availability period does not define retention of every record held by Render or other providers.

For AI suggestions, OpenAI may retain prompts, responses, and related metadata in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days under its standard policy. Longer retention may apply when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or others from harm. The Responses API also has a default 30-day application-state retention period, with response data stored for at least 30 days under that default. Retention depends on applicable API settings and OpenAI's policies. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default, unless the API customer explicitly opts in to sharing data.

Search and route requests may be retained in service-provider logs according to their policies and settings. This policy does not promise that all server records are deleted immediately after a request or after 7 days. Deleting a local profile does not automatically delete information previously sent to a provider.

Contact support with questions or deletion requests relating to online suggestions, searches, or routes. Include enough detail to identify the request without unnecessarily sharing sensitive information. We will explain what information we can identify and delete and any provider or legal limitations.

8. Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings and enter route locations manually. You can stop sending search, online suggestion, and route requests by not using those features. You can edit or delete local profiles in Avoid List. No app account is required. For privacy requests relating to support correspondence or the app's services, email the address above.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date will be updated on this page. Contact Honoka Kumagai at blockpeopleirl.support@gmail.com with any questions.
